**Authors:** Fernanda Sulantay Vargas, Kimia Sinaeian, and Amir Haji-Akbari

This preprint combines molecular dynamics, jumpy forward-flux sampling, and free-energy calculations to investigate how the treatment of long-range interactions affects phase stability and crystallization kinetics. It also develops a classical-nucleation-theory framework for estimating full-potential nucleation rates from finite-cutoff simulations.

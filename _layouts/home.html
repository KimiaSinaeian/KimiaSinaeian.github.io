About

Molecular science, from mechanism to design

Understanding ice at the molecular scale.

I’m a PhD candidate in Chemical Engineering at Yale University and a member of the Computational Soft Matter group. My research explores ice nucleation and the molecules that control it.

I’m especially interested in molecular modeling, enhanced sampling, scientific computing, and machine learning for challenges in biotechnology and materials science.

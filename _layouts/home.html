Research

Research themes

Following the molecular story of ice.

I use molecular simulation, rare-event sampling, and machine learning to understand how molecular interactions govern crystallization and protein recognition. My current research focuses on ice nucleation and antifreeze proteins, combining mechanistic studies with the development of efficient computational workflows.

01

Ice binding proteins

How do antifreeze proteins recognize and bind to ice? I develop computational workflows to map ice-binding sites and investigate how protein sequence, binding orientation, and side-chain conformations influence interactions with different ice surfaces.

02

Reaction coordinates for ice nucleation

Which molecular features best describe the progress of ice formation? I combine committor analysis and machine learning to evaluate reaction coordinates and identify how their construction affects their ability to capture nucleation progress.

03

Molecular interactions and nucleation kinetics

How sensitive are nucleation predictions to the interactions represented in a simulation? I investigate how the treatment of long-range interactions affects phase stability, nucleation barriers, and crystallization rates, helping establish more reliable simulation practices.
